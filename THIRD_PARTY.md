The isometric contribution geometry in `scripts/generate-contributions.py` is
adapted from [lowlighter/metrics, isocalendar](https://github.com/lowlighter/metrics/blob/366f8b9dfe3a59656c67d5dcad9950f59c9bc96d/source/plugins/isocalendar/index.mjs).
It is covered by the MIT license reproduced in `licenses/lowlighter-metrics.txt`.

The calendar reads the public contribution calendar for Haven-mao on GitHub.
GitHub contributions include eligible commits and other counted activity;
the graph does not represent a count of commits alone. The range covers about
180 days, rounded back to Sunday to match the reference's half-year layout.

Technology badges come from Shields.io. The streak card comes from
github-readme-streak-stats.
